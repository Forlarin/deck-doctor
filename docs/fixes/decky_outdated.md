# Decky Loader is out of date

Your Decky Loader is older than the latest release. Steam updates often change things Decky relies on, and older Decky versions then fail to show plugins or their menus.

## Fix
1. Tap **Open Decky settings to update**. Or: Quick Access → Decky (plug icon) → ⚙ → **General** → check for updates.
2. Install the update. Decky restarts. All plugins and their settings are kept.

If Decky won't open at all, re-run the official installer from [decky.xyz](https://decky.xyz) in Desktop Mode.
